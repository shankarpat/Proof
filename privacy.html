Privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

Proof is a free app for your priorities, steps, milestones, notes and photos. It is built so that your information stays with you.

What Proof collects

If you don't sign in, nothing. Everything stays on your device, and we never receive it.

Signing in is optional. If you sign in to sync between devices, Proof stores:

  • Your email address, so you can sign in with a code.
  • Your sky: your priorities, steps, milestones, notes, reflections, settings and photos, so they can appear on each device where you sign in.

That's all. Proof has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your information, and we don't read or use it for anything except syncing it back to you.

Where your information is kept

  • In your browser or on your phone: everything is saved in your browser's own storage on that device. Clearing your browser's site data for Proof deletes it.
  • In the Windows app: everything is saved in Proof's folder in your Windows user profile on that computer.
  • If you sign in: a copy is also kept in your Proof account, stored with Supabase, the service that runs Proof's accounts. Your account is protected so that only you, signed in, can reach your sky and photos. They are encrypted while being sent and while stored.

You can also move everything yourself without an account: use Settings, then Save a backup, and Restore a backup on the other device. A backup is a file you control, and it includes your photos, so keep it somewhere private.

Photos and passcodes

Photos you add stay on your device, and are also stored in your account if you sign in. The photo passcode hides locked photos from other people using your device. It is a privacy screen, not encryption.

Deleting your information

In Settings, choose Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, your email address and everything synced to it, including photos. Signing out lets you choose to clear the device too. Without an account, clearing your browser's site data for Proof, or uninstalling the Windows app, removes it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Windows download is hosted on GitHub. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses when pages or files are requested. See GitHub's privacy statement. Proof itself loads no outside fonts, scripts or trackers.

Links to other sites

Proof links to a few outside sites, such as a directory of support helplines and, if you choose, a tip page. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Proof isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly let them create accounts. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
