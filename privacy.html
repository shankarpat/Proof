Privacy policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

Proof is a free app for your priorities, steps, milestones, notes and photos. It is built so that your information stays with you.

What Proof collects

Nothing. Proof has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't receive, store, sell or share anything you put into the app.

Where your information is kept

  • In your browser or on your phone: everything is saved in your browser's own storage on that device. Clearing your browser's site data for Proof deletes it.
  • In the Windows app: everything is saved in Proof's folder in your Windows user profile on that computer.

Your information doesn't sync between devices. To move it, use Settings, then Save a backup, and Restore a backup on the other device. A backup is a file you control, and it includes your photos, so keep it somewhere private.

Photos and passcodes

Photos you add stay on your device. The photo passcode hides locked photos from other people using your device. It is a privacy screen, not encryption.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Windows download is hosted on GitHub. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses when pages or files are requested. See GitHub's privacy statement. Proof itself loads no outside fonts, scripts or trackers.

Links to other sites

Proof links to a few outside sites, such as a directory of support helplines and, if you choose, a tip page. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Proof doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
