How it works

Small promises, kept on purpose.

  1. Name what matters

    Write down a priority and finish the sentence "I am someone who…". That belief is what every step is quietly proving.

    My health. I am someone who moves with love.

  2. Keep small promises

    Add the steps that come from it: every day, certain days, or a few times a week. Each one can have a tiny version for low-energy days.

    Go for a walk. Or just put on your shoes.

  3. Watch your sky fill

    Every step you check off becomes a star in that priority's color. Stars join into constellations you can name. Nothing you did is ever lost.

    The Runner, 51 stars and counting.

Built for the hard days

Missing a day doesn't break anything.

There are no streaks to lose and no red numbers. When you come back after time away, Proof counts it as a comeback, because returning is the whole skill.

  • Tap "Having a hard day?" and today shrinks to one tiny step. Rest counts as showing up.
  • Proof shows you your own evidence: past comebacks, notes you wrote, moments you were proud of.
  • A new comforting quote each time, and a gentle link to real support if it's more than a hard day.

Look back and see it

Your progress, gathered up.

See what you did today, this week, this month and this year, where your energy went, and every time you got back up.

Milestones that let you try again

Pick a goal and a date. If you reach it, you celebrate. If not, you choose a new date and every attempt is kept.

Proud moments

Good things that weren't on any list, like setting a boundary or asking for help, earn their own kind of star.

Letters to future you

Write a letter that stays sealed until a date, a milestone, or your next hard day.

Private photos

Track any kind of change in pictures. Blur or lock single photos or whole categories with a passcode.

On this day

See what you did a week, a month, or a year ago, so you notice how far you've come.

A sky that's yours

Sunrise, golden hour, sunset or midnight, and star shapes you choose. A new quote every day.

Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

Proof has no accounts and no servers holding your information. What you write, check off and photograph is saved on the device you use.
